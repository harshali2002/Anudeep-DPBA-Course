Module 2 Day 4
Data Cleaning and Validation

Part 1 -> The Detective

rule - if you have bad data, you make bad reports, your boss makes bad decisions like firing you.

scenario - 5k or 10k + rows we need to find out the duplicates and remove duplicate rows when required.

to highlight duplicates we use conditional formatting
to purge the duplicates we use remove duplicates available in data tab.

Part 2 -> The Separator (Text to columns & Flash Fill)

Scenario - you have the names in the format "smith,john", you need the values in 2 different columns

method -> text to columns or flash fill

text to columns takes 5 clicks and will be preferred to use with multiple columns

flash fill will be preferred to use for one or two columns

Part 3 -> The Shield (Data Validation & Dropdowns)

Restricting Input - make users pick values from a list or a dedicated format


Part 4 -> The Surgeon (Find/Replace and go to special)

Find and replace - it helps to replace the wrongly placed values in the data. - ctrl+h

go to special - first cell, last cell, blanks and etc - ctrl+g
